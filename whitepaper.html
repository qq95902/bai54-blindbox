54 张扑克牌构成的每日盲盒游戏：随机开牌、每日开奖、卡牌合成与玩家交易。

1. 项目概览

BAI / 54（binanceAI 盲盒）把每日随机抽牌、盲盒收藏、卡牌合成和玩家间交易组合为一套游戏循环。牌面由四种花色、A 至 2 共 52 张普通牌，加大王和小王组成。

10 USDT单盒价格
10 盒 / 钱包 / 日北京时间 00:00 重置额度
2,000 盒 / 日每日销售上限
14 个中奖牌面12 张随机普通牌 + 大小王

BAI 是本项目的代币代码。项目与 Binance 无隶属、授权、赞助或背书关系；“Binance Agentic Wallet”仅描述计划支持的钱包接入方式。

2. 创世私募份额

$200 × 50 份创世档位 A
$500 × 50 份创世档位 B
100%计划投入初始流动性池

两档份额各限 50 份。私募资金计划全部用于初始流动性池；LP 凭证归属、锁定安排、认购者权益和付款流程以最终公开方案为准。

3. 每日盲盒与开奖

  1. 玩家连接指定钱包并支付 USDT 或按公开报价折算的 BNB，每盒 10 USDT；每个钱包每天最多买 10 盒，全局每天最多售出 2,000 盒。
  2. 每盒随机获得 54 种牌面中的一张。每日北京时间 00:00 结算上一轮并重置购买额度；开出的牌面作为独立卡牌进入卡册，盲盒凭证完成结算。
  3. 每轮从 52 张普通牌中无放回抽出 12 张中奖牌，再固定加入大王和小王，共 14 张。当天牌面变动，奖池按牌面匹配结果结算。
  4. 每日销售额的 22%用于中奖 USDT 奖励：普通牌合计 20%，大王 1%，小王 1%。同一中奖牌面有多个持有人时，该牌面预算由符合条件的中奖盒均分；无人命中的份额滚入下一轮奖池。
  5. 开奖结果、玩家牌面、中奖状态和实际到账金额均写入历史记录。中奖卡用于领取对应 USDT 奖励，不可出售；未中奖卡进入卡册，可在规则允许时出售或参与合成。

4. 每日资金分配

G = 当日成功付款的盲盒数量 × 10 USDT
中奖预算 = G × 22%（普通牌 20% + 大王 1% + 小王 1%）
流动性预算 = G × 78%

流动性预算全部进入自动 LP 流程：一半 USDT 用于买入 BAI，另一半 USDT 与买入的 BAI 配对加入池子。实际执行应公开成交价、滑点保护、DEX 池型、LP 凭证归属及锁定安排；同一笔资金不得同时计入中奖预算或运营收入。

5. BAI 代币与奖励库存

BAI 采用固定总量模型，不设置管理员任意增发功能。当前规划总量为 2,000,000 BAI。

用途比例数量说明
初始流动性20%400,000与初始 USDT 配对；LP 凭证归属及锁定安排公开
游戏奖励库存80%1,600,000未中奖奖励、邀请奖励及奖励期销毁

奖励期内，每个未中奖盲盒按结算参考价领取 1 USDT 等值 BAI，并从 80%奖励库存销毁等值 BAI；邀请奖励从这份玩家奖励中拆分。80%奖励库存发放并销毁完毕后，盲盒销售与 USDT 中奖结算继续；未中奖卡不再产生新的 BAI 奖励，并按后续收藏、转让与市场规则处理。

BAI 交易税与 SR 奖励池

BAI 买卖交易税由合约按成交额收取，并按预先公开的比例分配；其中 SR 奖励池来源于实际收到的交易税，项目运营账户及销毁比例须在部署前公布并锁定。普通钱包互转、卡牌交易、合成费及奖励领取是否计税，应以最终合约规则为准。

每张 SR 或 SSR 卡在每日快照中计 1 份权重；持有人按其快照权重占全部有效权重的比例，领取 SR 奖励池中实际收到的 BAI。未领取金额滚存至后续周期。奖励只来自已实际收取的交易税：每期按快照权重分配该周期实际进入奖池的 BAI。

6. 卡牌收藏与合成

开奖卡牌保留在卡册。中奖卡不可出售或合成；未中奖卡可出售或用于合成。合成卡可继续参与下一等级合成，但仍不可出售。

材料等级成功结果BAI 合成费

任意 4 张 S 级非中奖卡50% 成功，生成 1 张 SR25 BAI

每次选择 4 张同等级、未中奖且未挂单的卡牌资产（包括合成卡）；不要求点数或花色相同，玩家可从该等级全部合格卡牌中自由选择。成功时从四张材料卡的牌面中随机继承一张并升至下一等级。失败时 4 张材料卡全部销毁，不产生新卡；BAI 合成费仍销毁且不退。BNB 网络 Gas 由玩家钱包另行支付，金额随网络状态变化，不属于项目收入。SSR 为最高等级。

SR 与 SSR 各自获得 SR 奖励池资格，每张权重相同。合成结果和尝试记录应公开可查；上线合约须采用链上可验证随机数决定成功与否及继承牌面。

7. 卡牌挂单与撮合

  • 未中奖卡牌持有人可设置 USDT 标价并创建公开卖单；中奖卡和合成卡不能出售。
  • 买家按标价即时成交并支付 USDT，卡牌所有权转至买家。卖家可在成交前撤单。
  • 已挂单卡牌不能合成；合成中卡牌不能挂单。成交、撤单与资产变更均写入记录。
  • 链上市场需公布托管方式、交易费、支付资产、合约权限和成交索引规则。

8. 钱包邀请机制

购买至少一个盲盒的钱包获得专属邀请链接。受邀地址首次连接时绑定邀请关系；每个钱包只能绑定一次，不允许自邀、循环关系或修改既有关系。

层级比例基数
一级5%该盒基础 BAI 奖励
二级3%同一基础奖励
三级1%同一基础奖励

完整三级最多分配基础奖励的 9%，其余归盲盒玩家；缺失层级份额返回玩家。邀请奖励从玩家的基础奖励中拆分，不额外增发。

9. 随机公平、钱包与安全

  • 每轮销售结束后冻结参与集合，再请求支持目标链的可验证随机数；据此无放回抽出 12 张普通中奖牌，并加入固定大小王。
  • 发布轮次、随机请求标识、开奖结果、结算账目和税费分配记录。随机服务异常时按公开超时流程暂停结算，不允许管理员自行重抽或选择结果。
  • 合约应将销售分账、奖励发放、合成成功率、牌面继承、库存变化和卡牌权限写入链上；完成独立安全审计后再启用资产功能。
  • 须公开部署地址、税费收款地址、金库多签与权限、LP 凭证安排、暂停条件及恢复流程。钱包连接本身不等于购买授权。

10. 上线准备

  1. 锁定参数和完整资金流，完成合约与前端开发。
  2. 在 BNB Chain 验证随机开奖、分账、税费快照、合成失败、退款边界及市场撮合。
  3. 完成独立审计、法律审查、权限治理和紧急预案。
  4. 发布合约地址、审计报告、资金和税费参数后，再开放链上资产功能。

1. Project Overview

BAI / 54 (binanceAI Blind Box) combines a daily random draw, blind-box collecting, card synthesis, and player-to-player listings. The deck contains four suits and 52 standard cards from A through 2, plus the Big and Small Jokers.

Each tier is limited to 50 allocations. All private-sale proceeds are planned for the initial liquidity pool. LP receipt ownership, lock terms, participant entitlements, and payment steps will follow the final published plan.

3. Daily Boxes and Draw

  1. Players connect the designated wallet and pay USDT or BNB converted at the published quote. Each box costs 10 USDT; each wallet may buy up to 10 boxes per day, with a global cap of 2,000.
  2. Each box receives one random face from the 54-card deck. At 00:00 Beijing time, the round settles and the daily quota resets. The revealed face becomes a separate card in the collection.
  3. Each round selects 12 winning standard cards without replacement from the 52-card deck, then adds both Jokers, for 14 winning faces. The winning faces change each day.
  4. Twenty-two percent of daily sales funds USDT prizes: 20% for standard cards, 1% for the Big Joker, and 1% for the Small Joker. If multiple boxes match one winning face, they split that face’s allocation. Unclaimed amounts roll into the next round.
  5. Cards remain in the collection after settlement. Winning cards cannot be sold; non-winning cards can be sold or used for synthesis. The history records the face, round, outcome, and actual payout. Winning cards settle to their USDT reward and cannot be sold; non-winning cards enter the collection and may be sold or synthesized under the applicable rules.

4. Daily Fund Allocation

G = boxes successfully paid for that day × 10 USDT
Prize budget = 22% × G (20% standard cards + 1% Big Joker + 1% Small Joker)
Liquidity budget = 78% × G

The full liquidity budget enters an automated LP process. Half of the USDT budget buys BAI; the remaining half is paired with the acquired BAI and added to the pool. Execution should disclose price impact protection, DEX pool type, LP receipt ownership, and lock terms. Funds cannot be counted as prizes or operating income at the same time.

5. BAI Token and Reward Inventory

BAI is planned with a fixed supply of 2,000,000 BAI and no discretionary mint function.

During the reward phase, a non-winning box receives BAI worth 1 USDT at the published settlement reference price. An equal value of BAI is burned from the 80% game inventory. Referral shares come out of the player’s base award. After the 80% reward allocation has been fully distributed and burned, box sales and USDT prize settlement continue. Non-winning cards no longer generate new BAI rewards and follow the subsequent collection, transfer, and marketplace rules.

BAI Trading Tax and SR Reward Pool

The contract collects a BAI trading tax on buys and sells and distributes it according to a disclosed allocation. The SR reward pool is funded from trading tax actually received; the operating-treasury and burn shares must be published and locked before deployment. Tax treatment for ordinary transfers, card trades, synthesis fees, and reward claims follows the final contract rules.

Each SR or SSR card counts as one share at the daily snapshot. A holder may claim the portion of BAI actually received by the SR pool in proportion to their snapshot shares. Unclaimed amounts roll forward. Each period distributes the BAI actually received by the pool according to the snapshot weights.

6. Card Collection and Synthesis

Revealed cards remain in the collection. Winning cards cannot be sold or synthesized. Non-winning cards can be listed or synthesized; synthesized cards may be used in a later synthesis but cannot be sold.

Eligible materialsSuccessful resultBAI fee
Any four eligible R cards50% chance to create one S card10 BAI
Any four eligible S cards50% chance to create one SR card25 BAI
Any four eligible SR cards50% chance to create one SSR card50 BAI

Select any four non-winning, unlisted cards of the same rarity, including synthesized cards. Their rank and suit do not need to match; players may freely choose from all eligible cards at that tier. On success, the new card randomly inherits one of the four source faces and advances one tier. On failure, all four materials are burned and no new card is created. The BAI fee is also burned and is not refunded. The player separately pays variable BNB network gas through their wallet; gas is not project income. SSR is the highest tier.

Each SR and SSR card receives one share of the SR reward pool. Synthesis outcomes and attempts should be publicly verifiable. The deployed contract should use verifiable on-chain randomness for success and face inheritance.

7. Card Listings and Matching

  • Holders may set a USDT price and create a public listing for a non-winning card. Winning and synthesized cards cannot be sold.
  • A buyer pays the asking price and ownership transfers to the buyer. A seller can cancel before a fill.
  • A listed card cannot be synthesized, and a card being synthesized cannot be listed. Fills, cancellations, and ownership changes are recorded.
  • The on-chain marketplace must disclose escrow, fees, payment assets, contract permissions, and trade indexing.

8. Wallet Referrals

A wallet that buys at least one box receives a unique referral link. A referral binds when the new wallet first connects; each wallet may bind once. Self-referrals, referral loops, and edits to an existing relationship are prohibited.

All three levels receive at most 9% of the base award; the player receives the remainder. Missing levels return to the player. Referral shares are carved out of the base award and are not minted on top.

9. Randomness, Wallets, and Security

  • After each round closes, freeze the eligible box set and request verifiable randomness supported by the target chain. Draw 12 standard winning cards without replacement, then add both fixed Jokers.
  • Publish the round, randomness request ID, results, settlement ledger, and fee distributions. If the randomness service fails, pause settlement under a public timeout rule; administrators cannot choose a replacement result.
  • Contracts should enforce fund allocation, payouts, synthesis odds, inherited card faces, inventory changes, and card permissions. Complete an independent audit before enabling assets.
  • Publish contract addresses, tax destinations, treasury multisig and permissions, LP receipt terms, pause conditions, and recovery procedures. Wallet connection alone is not purchase authorization.

10. Launch Readiness

  1. Finalize parameters and fund flows; complete contract and frontend development.
  2. Validate draws, accounting, tax snapshots, synthesis failures, edge cases, and marketplace matching on BNB Chain.
  3. Complete independent security and legal reviews, permission governance, and incident procedures.
  4. Publish contract addresses, audit reports, and final parameters before enabling on-chain assets.
