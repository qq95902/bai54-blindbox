3. 每日盲盒与开奖

  1. 玩家通过 Binance Agentic Wallet 连接网站并以 USDT 付款；每盒 10 USDT，每钱包每天最多 10 盒，全局每天最多售出 2,000 盒。
  2. 每盒从 54 种牌面中独立随机开出一张，允许重复。每日北京时间 00:00 结算上一轮并重置额度；开出的卡牌 NFT 留在卡册，盲盒凭证结算后消耗。
  3. 每轮从 52 张普通牌中无放回抽出 12 张中奖牌，再固定加入大王和小王，共 14 张。单盒理论命中率为 14/54 ≈ 25.9%。
  4. 每日销售额的 22%用于中奖 USDT 奖励：普通牌合计 20%，平均分给 12 个中奖普通牌面（每面约为销售额的 1.667%）；大王 1%，小王 1%。同一牌面多人中奖时均分该牌预算；无人命中的份额滚入下一轮。
  5. 开奖结果、牌面、中奖状态和实收金额记入历史。中奖卡不可出售或合成；未中奖卡可出售或参与合成。

4. 每日资金分配

G = 当日成功付款的盲盒数量 × 10 USDT
中奖预算 = G × 22%（普通牌 20% + 大王 1% + 小王 1%）
流动性预算 = G × 78%（39% 买入 BAI + 39% USDT 配对）

流动性预算的 39% 用于买入 BAI；另 39% USDT 与买到的 BAI 加入 PancakeSwap V2 池。LP 凭证锁定 365 天。买入与加池执行链上滑点保护；未达到保护条件的资金保留在合约账本。

BAI 买卖税与 SR/SSR 分红

DEX 买入和卖出各收 2% BAI 税：1 个百分点进入公开运营金库，1 个百分点进入 SR/SSR 分红池。普通转账、卡牌交易、合成费和奖励领取不收此项税；税率与收款地址部署后固定。

每张 SR 或 SSR 卡每日快照计 1 份权重，按权重分配分红池实际收到的 BAI。未领取金额滚存。分红只来自实际收到的交易税；交易量低时分红可能很少或为零，不承诺固定收益。

  • 已挂单卡牌不能合成；合成中卡牌不能挂单。成交、撤单与资产变更均写入记录。

1. Project Overview

3. Daily Boxes and Draw

  1. Players connect through Binance Agentic Wallet and pay in USDT. Each box costs 10 USDT; each wallet may buy up to 10 boxes per day, with a global cap of 2,000.
  2. Each box independently reveals one random face from the 54-card deck; duplicates are possible. At 00:00 Beijing time, the round settles and the quota resets. The card NFT remains in the collection; the box is consumed after settlement.
  3. Each round selects 12 winning standard cards without replacement from the 52-card deck, then adds both Jokers, for 14 winning faces. The theoretical hit chance per box is 14/54, or about 25.9%.
  4. Twenty-two percent of daily sales funds USDT prizes: 20% for standard cards, split evenly across the 12 winning standard faces (about 1.667% of sales per face), 1% for the Big Joker, and 1% for the Small Joker. Boxes matching one winning face split that face’s allocation. Unmatched face allocations roll into the next round.
  5. Results, card faces, outcomes, and actual payouts are recorded. Winning cards cannot be sold or synthesized; non-winning cards can be sold or synthesized.

4. Daily Fund Allocation

G = boxes successfully paid for that day × 10 USDT
Prize budget = 22% × G
Liquidity budget = 78% × G (39% to buy BAI + 39% USDT paired)

Thirty-nine percent of gross sales buys BAI; another 39% in USDT is paired with the acquired BAI in a PancakeSwap V2 pool. LP receipts are locked for 365 days. Swaps and liquidity additions use on-chain slippage protection; funds remain in the contract ledger if protection conditions are not met.

5. BAI Token and Reward Inventory

BAI has a fixed supply of 2,000,000 BAI, minted once at deployment with no minting authority.

Each non-winning box receives BAI worth 1 USDT, while an equal value is burned from the reward inventory. Amounts use one daily reference price: the prior 24-hour BAI/USDT TWAP. New rounds do not open when total pool value is below 50,000 USDT equivalent or TWAP data is insufficient. Referral shares come out of the player’s 1 USDT base award. If inventory cannot cover full awards, the remainder is distributed proportionally. Once exhausted, BAI awards and matching burns stop while box sales and USDT prizes continue.

BAI Buy/Sell Tax and SR/SSR Distributions

DEX buys and sells each incur a 2% BAI tax: one percentage point goes to the public operating treasury and one percentage point funds the SR/SSR distribution pool. No such tax applies to ordinary transfers, card trades, synthesis fees, or reward claims. The rate and destinations are fixed at deployment.

Each SR or SSR card counts as one share at the daily snapshot. The pool distributes only BAI actually received, proportionally by share count. Unclaimed amounts roll forward. Distributions depend on tax actually received; low trading volume may mean a small or zero payout. No fixed return is promised.

6. Card Collection and Synthesis

Winning cards cannot be sold or synthesized. Any four non-winning, unlisted cards can be combined regardless of rank, suit, or tier. Synthesized cards cannot be sold but may be used again as materials.

Highest input tierSuccessful resultBAI fee
R50% chance to create one S card10 BAI
S50% chance to create one SR card25 BAI
SR or SSR50% chance to create one SSR card50 BAI

On success, the result advances one tier above the highest input tier, capped at SSR, and receives a newly randomized face from the 54-card set. Once randomness returns, all four materials and the BAI fee are burned whether synthesis succeeds or fails. Players pay network gas separately. If VRF randomness is not returned within 7 days, anyone may trigger cancellation; the materials and BAI fee are refunded to their owner, but network gas is not refunded. Each SR and SSR card counts as one distribution share.

Verifiable randomness determines success or failure and the new card face.

7. Card Listings and Matching

  • Holders may set a USDT price and create a public listing for a non-winning card. Winning and synthesized cards cannot be sold.
  • A buyer pays the asking price and ownership transfers to the buyer. The V1 marketplace fee is 0%, so the seller receives the full price. A seller can cancel before a fill.
  • A listed card cannot be synthesized, and a card being synthesized cannot be listed. Fills, cancellations, and ownership changes are recorded.

8. Wallet Referrals

A wallet that buys at least one box receives a unique referral link. A referral binds when the new wallet completes its first purchase; each wallet may bind once. Self-referrals, referral loops, and edits to an existing relationship are prohibited.

9. Randomness, Wallets, and Contract Permissions

  • Chainlink VRF v2.5 provides verifiable randomness. Publish round IDs, request IDs, draw results, and settlement ledgers; administrators cannot reroll or choose results.
  • The website offers Binance Agentic Wallet as its connection flow. A public contract cannot identify which wallet app signed a transaction; the contract enforces address, quota, and payment rules.
  • Core parameters cannot be upgraded or changed by an administrator. An emergency pause may stop new purchases only; it cannot freeze accrued USDT prizes, BAI claims, or card assets. The public treasury uses a multisig.
