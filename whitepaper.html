2. 每日盲盒与开奖

  1. 每盒价值 10 USDT，可使用 USDT 或按页面报价支付等值 BNB；每钱包每天最多 10 盒，全局每天最多售出 2,000 盒。首次使用 USDT 购买时，钱包会先弹出代币授权确认，再弹出购买确认。
  2. 每盒由 Chainlink VRF 随机分配 54 种主题卡面之一，允许重复。每天北京时间 00:00 后销售轮次关闭并重置购买额度；任何人或自动化 Keeper 必须提交开奖请求、读取随机数、分批结算及最终结算交易。VRF 与链上结算完成前，卡牌不会揭晓，奖励不会结算。
  3. 每轮从 52 种常规卡面中无放回抽出 12 种，再固定加入 BTC 和 ETH 两种特别卡面，共 14 种中奖卡面。单盒理论命中率为 14/54 ≈ 25.9%。
  4. 每日销售额的 22%用于中奖 USDT 奖励：常规卡面合计 20%，平均分给 12 种中奖卡面（每种约为销售额的 1.667%）；BTC 和 ETH 各占 1%。同一卡面多人中奖时均分该卡面预算；无人命中的份额滚入下一轮。特别卡仍以 USDT 结算，不发 BTC 或 ETH。
  5. 开奖结果、卡面、中奖状态和实收金额记入历史。中奖卡不可出售或合成；未中奖卡可出售或参与合成。

3. 每日资金分配

G = 当日成功付款的盲盒数量 × 10 USDT
中奖预算 = G × 22%（常规卡面 20% + BTC 1% + ETH 1%）
邀请返佣 = 有效直属邀请购买额 × 30%（每盒 3 USDT）
流动性预算 = G × 78% − 当日邀请返佣（全部由流动性预算承担）

无邀请关系的购买，其 78% 全部计入流动性预算；有邀请关系的购买，每盒从该预算拨出 3 USDT 作为直属邀请人的待领取返佣，剩余 4.8 USDT 计入流动性预算。流动性预算用于按 1:1 价值买入 BAI 并加入 PancakeSwap V2 池；LP 凭证锁定 365 天。未领取返佣作为合约负债保留，不会重复计入奖池或流动性。

BAI 买卖税与 SR+ 分红

DEX 买入和卖出各收 2% BAI 税：1 个百分点进入公开运营金库，1 个百分点进入 SR 及以上等级分红池。普通转账、卡牌交易、合成费和奖励领取不收此项税；税率与收款地址部署后固定。

每张 SR、SSR 或 SSSR 卡每日快照计 1 份权重，按权重分配分红池实际收到的 BAI。未领取金额滚存。分红只来自实际收到的交易税；交易量低时分红可能很少或为零，不承诺固定收益。

8. 钱包邀请机制

购买至少一个盲盒的钱包获得专属邀请链接。受邀地址首次成功购买时绑定直属邀请人；每个钱包只能绑定一次，绑定后不可修改。禁止自邀。邀请奖励只有一级：直属邀请人每当受邀钱包成功购买一个 10 USDT 盲盒，获得 3 USDT 返佣；一次购买多盒按盒数累计。免费领取、卡牌交易和合成不产生邀请返佣。

返佣按 USDT 记账，在购买交易确认后进入直属邀请人的待领取余额，邀请人可单独发起领取；未中奖卡的 BAI 奖励完整发给卡牌持有人，返佣不再从 BAI 奖励中拆分。无有效邀请人的购买不会产生返佣，相应资金留在流动性预算中。

2. Daily Boxes and Draw

  1. Each box costs 10 USDT and can be paid for in USDT or the equivalent BNB at the displayed quote. Each wallet may buy up to 10 boxes per day, with a global cap of 2,000. For the first USDT purchase, the wallet prompts for token approval, then prompts for the purchase.
  2. Chainlink VRF assigns each box one of the 54 themed faces; duplicates are possible. The sales round closes and purchase quotas reset at 00:00 Beijing time. A person or automated Keeper must submit the draw request, publish fulfilled randomness, settle boxes in batches, and finalize the round. Cards are not revealed and rewards are not settled until VRF and these transactions complete.
  3. Each round selects 12 standard faces without replacement from the 52-face set, then adds the BTC and ETH special faces, for 14 winning faces. The theoretical hit chance per box is 14/54, or about 25.9%.
  4. Twenty-two percent of daily sales funds USDT prizes: 20% for standard faces, split evenly across the 12 winning faces (about 1.667% of sales per face), plus 1% for BTC and 1% for ETH. Boxes matching one face split that face’s allocation. Unmatched allocations roll into the next round. Special faces are paid in USDT, not BTC or ETH.
  5. Results, card faces, outcomes, and actual payouts are recorded. Winning cards cannot be sold or synthesized; non-winning cards can be sold or synthesized.

3. Daily Fund Allocation

G = successfully paid boxes for the day × 10 USDT
Prize budget = 22% × G (20% standard faces + 1% BTC + 1% ETH)
Referral commission = 30% of eligible direct invite purchases (3 USDT per box)
Liquidity budget = 78% × G − referral commission (both funded from the liquidity allocation)

Purchases without a valid referrer contribute the full 78% to liquidity. For a referred purchase, 3 USDT per box is reserved as claimable commission for the direct inviter and the remaining 4.8 USDT per box contributes to liquidity. The liquidity budget is used to acquire and pair BAI in the PancakeSwap V2 pool; LP receipts are locked for 365 days. Unclaimed commissions remain a contract liability and are excluded from prize and liquidity accounting.

4. BAI Token and Reward Inventory

BAI has a fixed supply of 2,000,000 BAI, minted once at deployment with no minting authority. Reward inventories total 80% (1,600,000 BAI): 985,000 BAI for the paid game and 615,000 BAI for free claims. Separate contracts manage these balances; they cannot be reassigned between campaigns.

Each non-winning box has a target base award worth 1 USDT in BAI, with an equal value burned. The BAI/USDT TWAP at the start of the round determines the token amount and is fixed for that round. Direct referral commission is paid separately in USDT and is not deducted from the BAI award. The contract sets one uniform actual award amount for all losing boxes in the round based on available inventory; if inventory is insufficient, every losing box receives the same reduced amount rather than a first-come allocation. After inventory is exhausted, new rounds provide no BAI award or matching burn, while box sales and USDT prizes may continue.

BAI Buy/Sell Tax and SR+ Distributions

DEX buys and sells each incur a 2% BAI tax: one percentage point goes to the public operating treasury and one percentage point funds the SR+ distribution pool. No such tax applies to ordinary transfers, card trades, synthesis fees, or reward claims. The rate and destinations are fixed at deployment.

Each SR, SSR, or SSSR card counts as one share at the daily snapshot. The pool distributes only BAI actually received, proportionally by share count. Unclaimed amounts roll forward. Distributions depend on tax actually received; low trading volume may mean a small or zero payout. No fixed return is promised.

6. Card Collection and Synthesis

Winning cards cannot be sold or synthesized. Any four non-winning, unlisted cards can be combined regardless of theme, visual finish, or tier. Synthesized cards cannot be sold but may be used again as materials.

The player interface does not currently offer on-chain synthesis; the following describes the contract rules. On success, the result advances one tier above the highest input tier, capped at SSSR, and receives a randomly selected face from the 54 themed faces. Once randomness returns, all four materials and the BAI fee are burned whether synthesis succeeds or fails. Players pay network gas separately. If VRF randomness is not returned within 7 days, anyone may trigger cancellation; the materials and BAI fee are refunded to their owner, but network gas is not refunded. Each SR, SSR, and SSSR card counts as one distribution share.

The BTC and ETH special faces are assigned SSSR rarity and are always included in the daily winning set; their prizes are paid from the corresponding USDT face allocations.

7. Card Listings and Matching

The player interface does not currently offer on-chain marketplace trading; the following describes the contract rules.

  • Holders may set a USDT price and create a public listing for a non-winning card. Winning and synthesized cards cannot be sold.
  • A buyer pays the asking price and ownership transfers to the buyer. The V1 marketplace fee is 0%, so the seller receives the full price. A seller can cancel before a fill.
  • A listed card cannot be synthesized, and a card being synthesized cannot be listed. Fills, cancellations, and ownership changes are recorded.

8. Wallet Referrals

A wallet that buys at least one box receives a unique referral link. The direct inviter binds when a new wallet completes its first successful purchase; each wallet can bind once, and the binding cannot be changed. Self-referrals are rejected. The program has one level only: the direct inviter earns 3 USDT for each 10 USDT paid box purchased by the referred wallet. Multi-box purchases accrue commission per box. Free claims, card trades, and synthesis do not earn referral commission.

Commission is accounted in USDT after the purchase transaction is confirmed and added to the inviter’s claimable balance. The inviter claims it in a separate transaction. The non-winning card’s full BAI award goes to its holder; referral commission is no longer deducted from BAI rewards. Purchases without a valid inviter create no commission, and the corresponding funds remain in the liquidity allocation.

9. Randomness, Public Records, and Wallets

  • Chainlink VRF v2.5 supplies on-chain randomness for draws and synthesis. Draw requests, results, and settlements can be checked on a block explorer.
  • The website uses Binance Agentic Wallet pairing to verify an address. Pairing confirms wallet identity only; players review and confirm on-chain transactions in their wallet. The contract applies rules using the address and on-chain state.
  • After deployment and initialization, the main game parameters cannot be changed by an administrator. The contracts have no administrator pause switch. Refer to on-chain data and published records for deployed addresses, permissions, and treasury addresses.

5. Daily Free BAI Rewards

Free claims are separate from paid boxes. Each wallet may register once per day, with a daily cap of 3,000 and a campaign cap of 200,000. Rewards are BAI only: no card is opened, no NFT is minted, and no USDT is awarded.

195,000 claimsStandard reward: 1–3 BAI each
5,000 claimsSpecial reward: 4–6 BAI each

Registration does not pay immediately. After the day closes, the contract requests Chainlink VRF randomness and assigns rewards within the campaign’s remaining quota; an on-chain transaction is required to settle. If VRF fulfillment is delayed, anyone may submit a replacement request after 24 hours. To preserve the exact campaign-wide tier counts, settlement follows global registration order; later batches wait while an earlier day is unfulfilled and resume after the earlier draw is settled. Rewards first accrue as pending BAI. A wallet with no paid box purchase can withdraw them after buying at least one paid box.

The campaign reserves up to 615,000 BAI, enough to cover every claim at the maximum for its tier. After all 200,000 claims are settled and all pending rewards are withdrawn, anyone may trigger a burn of the BAI left in the campaign contract. The administrator cannot withdraw it.
