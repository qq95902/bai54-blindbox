首季固定 10,000 包，每包 5 张，共 50,000 张；售完不补。每张按所在档位的固定卡池无放回抽取，5 张不保证含稀有卡。全季卡数按稀有度封顶：R 43,525、SR 4,875、S 1,275、SS 288、SSS 37。75 个卡面在各稀有度内平均分配编号上限。当前锻造仅为本地演示，不铸 NFT；若上线链上锻造，必须从上述总量内预留编号。抽中编号即移出卡池，后续概率按剩余卡池计算。

Season 01 has a fixed supply of 10,000 packs, five cards per pack, and 50,000 cards total, with no restock. Each card is drawn without replacement from its tier pool; a five-card pack is not guaranteed to contain a rare card. Season card caps: 43,525 R, 4,875 SR, 1,275 S, 288 SS, and 37 SSS, distributed across 75 designs within each tier. The current forge is a local demo and does not mint NFTs; any on-chain forge must use serials within this total cap.

整包概率按无放回抽取计算：至少一张 SSS 的整包概率依次约为 0.040%、0.233%、0.996%、2.575%；整包 5 张全为 R 的概率依次约为 73.39%、47.04%、23.72%、7.76%。单张概率与整包概率不同，抽取后按剩余卡池更新。

Pack-level odds use draws without replacement: the chance of at least one SSS per pack is about 0.040%, 0.233%, 0.996%, and 2.575%; the chance that all five cards are R is about 73.39%, 47.04%, 23.72%, and 7.76%. Per-card and per-pack odds differ and update with the remaining pool.

一级邀请按有效直推实付金额固定计佣 10%；二级 3%，总佣金最高 13%。按 173,800 USDT 售罄销售额，54% 预算为 93,852 USDT。最终回收责任取决于逐卡报价表；报价表和最大负债尚未核定，因此该预算不代表已足额覆盖，也不能据此承诺固定回收。

Level 1 earns a fixed 10% of eligible direct purchases; level 2 earns 3%, for a 13% maximum total. At 173,800 USDT sellout gross, the 54% allocation is 93,852 USDT. Actual buyback liability depends on the per-card quote schedule, which is not yet finalized; this budget is not proof of full collateral and does not support a fixed buyback promise.

返佣只按有效成交计算，不构成固定收入或利润承诺。开售前须全额托管该批最大回收负债；售罄收入下该比例为 93,852 USDT。逐卡报价表与最大负债确认前，不得宣传固定回收。回收、返佣、流动性资金分别记账。

Commissions accrue only on eligible settled purchases and do not guarantee income or profit. The full maximum buyback liability must be escrowed before sale; the allocation would be 93,852 USDT at sellout. Do not advertise fixed buybacks until the per-card quote schedule and maximum liability are verified. Buyback, referral, and liquidity funds are segregated.

交易市场分红

Marketplace fee sharing

市场手续费单独入账，仅由结算时持有的 SSS 卡牌等权分配；转手前的收益归原持有人。费率与结算周期待定，卡包销售资金不参与。

Marketplace fees use a separate ledger and are shared equally by SSS holders at settlement. Accrued fees remain with the prior holder after transfer. Fee rate and settlement interval are pending; pack proceeds are excluded.

首批含 16 张 SSS。锻造卡需单独设发行上限；BAI 锻造成本和额度确定前，Demo 锻造卡不具备链上分红资格。

The first batch has 16 SSS cards. Forged cards need a separate supply cap; demo forge outputs are not fee-eligible until the BAI cost and cap are set.

BAI 代币

BAI token

总量 10% 预留私募；其余 90% 的分配、解锁与托管待定。BAI 锻造消耗量待定。现有 BAIToken 合约采用旧总量及流动性方案，不适用 TCG 2.0。

Ten percent of total supply is reserved for private sale. Allocation, vesting, and custody of the remaining 90%, and the BAI forge cost, are pending. The existing BAIToken contract uses the old supply and liquidity plan and is not suitable for TCG 2.0.
